Take the first step towards an exciting campus life!

Why Join Our Clubs?

Joining a student club is a great way to build skills, meet new friends, and make the most of your university experience.

Registration Steps

  1. Browse through our available club categories on the Clubs page.
  2. Fill out the online membership registration form below or visit our office.
  3. Attend an introductory orientation session.

Meeting Schedules

Most clubs hold regular weekly or bi-weekly meetings. Check the Events page for specific dates and times for orientation meetups.

Participation Guidelines

  • Maintain active participation in club activities and events.
  • Respect fellow members and foster an inclusive environment.
  • Adhere to university code of conduct at all times.
